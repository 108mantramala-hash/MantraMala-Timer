MantraMala - Your Peaceful Mantra Counter

A simple and elegant app for counting mantras, prayers, and affirmations during your spiritual practice.

✨ FEATURES

• TAP ANYWHERE TO COUNT
Simply tap anywhere on the screen to increment your count. No need to aim for small buttons.

• QUICK PRESETS
One-tap buttons for traditional counts:
  - 27 repetitions
  - 54 repetitions  
  - 108 repetitions (full mala)

• PEACEFUL BELL SOUND
Gentle chime plays when you reach your goal, helping you stay mindful without checking the screen.

• CUSTOMIZABLE GOALS
Set any target count in the settings to match your practice.

• AUTO-SAVE
Your count and goal are automatically saved. Pick up right where you left off.

• BEAUTIFUL DESIGN
Calming dark theme with elegant gradients designed for distraction-free practice.

🙏 PERFECT FOR

• Japa meditation
• Mantra chanting
• Prayer counting
• Affirmation practice
• Mindfulness exercises
• Any sacred repetitions

🌟 SIMPLE & FOCUSED

No ads. No tracking. No distractions.
Just you and your practice.

Start your mindful counting journey with MantraMala today.
